      How to use

      1. Add a class. Click Add class on the left and give it a name, such as Grade 8B.
      2. Add students. Paste your class list (numbered or not) or type names one by one and press Enter.
      3. Choose the curriculum. Pick IB MYP or DP, British (KS3, Cambridge or Pearson Edexcel IGCSE, AS and A Level), US (letter grades or NGSS standards-based), Indian (CBSE, ICSE and ISC) or your own local curriculum. Then choose the year or grade, the subject group and the course name used in comments, such as Physics. The app shows the matching year in other systems.
      4. Check the assessment structure. For IB DP, IGCSE, A Level, CBSE, ICSE and US classes, the app sets up the real papers, units or gradebook categories with their maximum marks and weightings, for example Paper 1A, Paper 1B, Paper 2 and the internal assessment for DP sciences. Choose SL or HL, Core or Extended, or Foundation or Higher where it applies. Click Components to edit them if your school differs.
      5. Enter marks. Choose how to enter achievement: raw marks for each paper, the curriculum’s own scale (MYP criteria 0 to 8, or grades such as A*, 7 or A1) or marks out of 100. Type them on each row, or click Paste marks and paste from Excel. The overall grade is worked out for you. Use Grade boundaries to match your school’s policy.
      6. Set student options. Use Set for whole class to give everyone a starting level in one click. Then click a student’s name to change their engagement, behaviour, progress, attendance, academic integrity, strengths, learner profile and ATL skills.
      7. Write comments. Choose a comment length at the top, then click Write all comments. Templates work offline. AI chatbot mode gives you a prompt to paste into Gemini, ChatGPT or Claude, then you paste the reply back.
      8. Check and send. Read and edit each comment, then copy it or use Export to move comments into ManageBac, Toddle or your school’s system.
      9. Keep a backup. Everything saves on this computer automatically. Use Save backup to keep a copy or move to another PC.

      Clear comments

      This removes written comments only. Students, marks and options stay. It can’t be undone, so save a backup first if you might need them.

      Export comments

      Works with ManageBac, Toddle, Sana, PowerSchool, Veracross, iSAMS, Engage and other reporting systems. Most systems let you paste each comment into the student’s report, so open the Excel file beside the system and copy across. If your school imports comments in bulk, send the Excel file to your coordinator.

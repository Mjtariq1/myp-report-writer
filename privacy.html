Privacy policy for MYP Report Writer

Last updated: 23 October 2026

MYP Report Writer is a free tool created by Muhammad Tariq to help teachers write end-of-term report comments.

What information the app stores

Everything you enter, including class names, student names, marks, options and comments, is stored only on your own device, in your browser’s local storage. It is never sent to the developer or to any server.

What information the app collects

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

AI chatbot mode

If you choose AI chatbot mode, the app creates a prompt that you can copy yourself and paste into a chatbot of your choice, such as Gemini, ChatGPT or Claude. The app does not send this information anywhere. If you paste it into a chatbot, that service’s own privacy policy applies, so follow your school’s rules on sharing student information.

Backups and exports

Backup and export files are saved only to a location you choose on your device.

Deleting your data

You can delete classes or comments inside the app at any time. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser data removes everything it stored.
